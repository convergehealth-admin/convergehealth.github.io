Critical findings

What the audit found.

Zero meta descriptions across all 12 pages

Every Google impression shows an auto-generated snippet. This directly reduces click-through rate — highest-ROI fix available.

Homepage title: "Home Page - Whiskey JYPSI"

Wastes the most valuable keyword slot in the entire site. Should target "Award-Winning Small Batch Bourbon."

4 pages missing H1 tags — /whiskey/, /jypsi-spirit/, /tribute/, /barrel-seekers/

Search engines can't identify the primary topic of these pages. Basic content hierarchy is broken.

No Product schema on any product page

Tribute, Explorer, Legacy, and Barrel Seekers all lack Product markup — no price, availability, or ratings in search results.

No FAQ schema, no question-phrased headings — site-wide

The brand has rich, answerable content but none of it is formatted for featured snippets or voice search. Invisible to answer engines.

No Person schema for Eric Church, Raj Alva, or Ari Sussman

AI engines can't confidently identify or cite the founders. Missing sameAs social links in Organization schema.

Brand pages are critically thin — /our-story/ (375 words), /jypsi-spirit/ (275 words)

These are the pages AI engines and journalists visit to understand the company. Both need 600+ words of substantive, factual content.

Image alt text: "starks logo" used across the entire site

A leftover placeholder from a prior theme. The brand name isn't reflected in its own logo alt text.

70+ press placements including Forbes, USA Today, Whisky Advocate, Esquire. 2024 Whiskey Maker of the Year. SF Double Gold, Ascot Platinum, 95-point Bottle Raiders score.

Named expert attribution. Ari Sussman is identified and quoted directly. Three named founders with real credentials — strongest E-E-A-T signal available in the celebrity spirits category.

Cherokee White Corn and cross-category blending are genuinely original narratives that differentiate from every competitor. AI engines prefer distinctive, citation-worthy content.

•  Tasting notes, product specs, and awards front-and-center — the exact factual density AI engines want to cite

•  Shoppable lifestyle grid + deep product sections — browse-and-buy impulse meets editorial storytelling

•  A design that matches the quality of a Double Gold whiskey — not a WordPress template
