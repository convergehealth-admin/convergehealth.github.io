The Strategic CIO’s Guide to Value Creation in PE-Backed Healthcare

Insights by Rod Jardine | Executive Strategy

In high-stakes clinical operations, technology is the primary engine of valuation. Throughout my career leading global teams for Fortune 50 health services leaders and PE-backed firms, I have seen how technical integrity directly dictates exit success.

I have leveraged these principles to stabilize infrastructure for a $3.3B strategic acquisition and manage global engineering organizations with $100M+ budgets.

The Tech Due Diligence Checklist

To de-risk an investment, you must move beyond the surface. A robust assessment focuses on documentation, security posture, and dev process maturity.

The Five Strategic Failure Modes

1. The "Code Theater" Mirage

Demos can mask significant technical debt. I often find platforms that lack the integrity to handle a 10x scaling event. True value lies in systems that survive diligence without immediate, massive reinvestment.

2. The Scalability Wall

Many platforms fail when pivoting to a multi-product strategy. Transitioning from a single clinical niche to a broad service provider requires shifting from monolithic to service-oriented architectures.

3. Governance as a Foundation

Frameworks like HIPAA, SOC 2, and HITRUST are not just burdens; they are "credibility armor". As a de facto Chief Security Officer, I've seen how governance protects the exit timeline.
